I absolutely loved this. I'm a long-time John Green fan, so not a huge surprise, but something about this captivated me from page one. I'm also nerdy about movies and Hollywood so that probably helped too. A beautiful story about love and finding your place in a world that wants you to just be a thing they can own and have opinions about instead of a person with all the messiness and nuance that entails.

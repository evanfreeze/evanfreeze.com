The idea of focusing on what you want someone to take away from your writing and doing everything you can to make that clear really came through for me here, but the tone of this is kind of wild. It feels very "look at us very smart business boys and how successful we are this is what you should do to be like us" which is off-putting. Also it feels like LLMs have been overly trained on this style of writing.
